The sixty-four gates

The sixty-four hexagrams of the I Ching are called “gates” in this system. What is here is what can be looked up: each gate’s hexagram, its image, which centre it belongs to, and which other gate it has to meet to become a channel. No interpretation. The interpretation is the book.
